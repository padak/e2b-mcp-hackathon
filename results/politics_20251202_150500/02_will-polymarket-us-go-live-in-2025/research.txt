**Polymarket US is on track to go live in 2025**, currently operating in beta mode with plans for an official launch before the end of the year. The platform has received key regulatory approvals, funding, and developed infrastructure for a regulated U.S. return.

Key points supporting this:

- **Regulatory approval:** Polymarket received an amended Order of Designation from the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), allowing it to operate as a fully regulated Designated Contract Market (DCM). This includes enhanced market supervision, clearing procedures, surveillance, and regulatory reporting (Part 16), which are prerequisites for official launch[3][5].

- **Operational beta:** Founder Shayne Coplan confirmed in November 2025 at a Cantor Fitzgerald conference that Polymarket US resumed operations in beta mode, providing limited event trading access to select users. The platform is in final preparation stages for full U.S. launch[2].

- **Legal resolution:** Polymarket settled past regulatory conflicts, including a $1.4 million fine with the CFTC in 2022 and concluding a DOJ investigation in July 2025. The CFTC withdrew prior claims, clearing the path for regulated U.S. market access[2].

- **Funding and valuation:** Polymarket secured a $2 billion investment from Intercontinental Exchange (operator of NYSE) and is negotiating further funding, with valuations estimated between $12 billion and $15 billion. This capital facilitates platform development and regulatory compliance[2][1].

- **Token plans:** After U.S. rollout, Polymarket plans to launch a native POLY utility token with an airdrop to active users projected for 2026, emphasizing long-term utility and community engagement rather than rushed release[1][6].

- **Partnerships and integrations:** Collaborations include an exclusive partnership with Yahoo Finance to embed prediction markets and integration with Google Finance’s AI updates, expanding user reach and visibility[2].

- **Competitive landscape:** Polymarket faces competition from new U.S. prediction market entrants like Robinhood, Gemini, and CME Group/FanDuel’s upcoming FanDuel Predicts app, suggesting a race to capture market share in 2025[2].

- **Market sentiment:** The Polymarket real-money prediction market question "Will Polymarket US go live in 2025?" has high trading volume (over $14 million) with bets favoring a "Yes" outcome. The market will resolve based on whether a real-money trade occurs on a regulated Polymarket-operated DCM by December 31, 2025[4].

**Factors influencing the outcome:**

- Completion and CFTC acceptance of Part 16 regulatory reporting tests, mandatory before official trading launch[4].

- Successful onboarding of intermediaries (broker-dealers, clearinghouses) and end users under new regulatory requirements[3].

- Competitive pressure to launch before rival platforms establish dominance.

- Maintaining regulatory compliance to avoid delays or additional investigations.

- Execution of planned token launch to incentivize liquidity and user engagement post-launch[1].

In summary, Polymarket has made substantial progress toward a regulated U.S. launch in 2025, with regulatory approval, operational beta, strong financial backing, and strategic partnerships. While some procedural steps remain, market and expert signals indicate a high likelihood it will go live by the end of 2025.
