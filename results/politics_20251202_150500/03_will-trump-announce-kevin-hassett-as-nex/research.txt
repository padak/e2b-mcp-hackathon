## Current Status

As of December 2, 2025, Kevin Hassett is the **strong frontrunner** to be announced as the next Federal Reserve chair, with the selection process unofficially nearing completion.[1] President Trump appears to favor his longtime adviser, and interviews with finalists have begun this week with Vice President JD Vance and senior White House staff.[1]

## Key Recent Developments

**Hassett's Strong Position**

Hassett meets Trump's two primary criteria for the role: loyalty and credibility with markets.[1] National Economic Council Director Hassett has publicly signaled his openness to the position, stating he would be "happy to serve" if selected.[2] He has emphasized that market conditions are favorable for a transition, noting that "interest rates went down" and the Treasury recently had "one of our best Treasury auctions ever."[2]

**Timeline for Announcement**

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent indicated that Trump could make the announcement before Christmas 2025 or in the new year, suggesting a decision timeline within weeks.[2] This means an announcement in 2025 remains quite possible given we are currently in early December.

**Market Response**

The markets have reacted positively to the prospect of Hassett's selection, with the anticipation of leadership change already reflected in recent financial performance.[2]

## Competing Candidates

Other serious contenders for the position include:[2]

- Federal Reserve Governor Michelle Bowman (also Fed vice chair for supervision)
- Rick Rieder (BlackRock's chief investment officer of global fixed income)
- Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller
- Former Federal Reserve Governor Kevin Warsh

Despite these alternatives, **Hassett is described as the clear favorite** according to recent reporting.[2]
