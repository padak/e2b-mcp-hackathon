## Current Nomination Probability

Christopher Waller's chances of being nominated as the next Federal Reserve Chair have fluctuated recently but remain competitive among the top candidates. As of late November 2025, betting markets show Waller with a **22% nomination probability on PredictIt**, up from 12% earlier in the month.[2] Other sources cite his probability at around **12% on Polymarket**.[1]

## Key Contenders and Market Position

Waller is currently the second or third most likely candidate, behind frontrunner Kevin Hassett. The nomination race includes five main candidates mentioned by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent:[2]

- **Kevin Hassett** (Director of National Economic Council): 53% probability on PredictIt, 72% on Polymarket[1][2]
- **Christopher Waller** (Federal Reserve Governor): 22% probability on PredictIt, 12% on Polymarket[1][2]
- **Kevin Warsh** (Former Federal Reserve Governor): 10-11% probability[1][2]
- **Michele Bowman** (Federal Reserve Governor): 1% probability[2]
- **Rick Rieder** (BlackRock CIO): Minor candidate[2]

## Waller's Strengths as a Candidate

**Dovish Monetary Policy Stance**: Waller has demonstrated support for interest rate cuts, which aligns with Trump's policy preferences. He led two benchmark rate cuts in 2025 and reaffirmed in a speech on November 17 that "an additional rate cut is necessary in December," citing a weakening job market.[2] In his cautious dovish approach, he stated: "We should implement interest rate cuts, but in a prudent manner—25 basis points at a time—while closely monitoring economic feedback."[1]

**Federal Reserve Experience**: As a current Federal Reserve Governor (having taken office in December 2020), Waller brings institutional credibility and extensive tenure within the Fed system.[1][4] He previously served as executive vice president and director of research at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis since 2009.[4]

**Recent Momentum**: Waller's nomination probability surged notably in late November after Treasury Secretary Bessent mentioned him as one of the two current Fed governors under consideration and after Waller disclosed in a Fox Business interview that he had "discussed the Fed Chair position with Bessent."[2] His recent willingness to publicly engage with the possibility has boosted his profile.

## Factors Working Against Waller's Nomination

**Hawkish Historical Record**: While Waller has adopted a more dovish stance recently, his core monetary policy views remain more conservative than Hassett's. In 2009, he warned that upside risks to inflation outweighed downside risks, and he expressed reservations during the 2010 debate over QE2.[1] This hawkish history may concern Trump, who favors aggressive rate cuts.

**Hassett's Dominance**: Kevin Hassett maintains a commanding lead in betting markets and is widely considered the frontrunner. According to Bloomberg reporting on November 25, government insiders stated that "Hassett has clearly taken the lead."[2] Hassett's stronger alignment with Trump's pro-growth, rate-cutting agenda and his status as a "Trump loyalist" give him significant advantages.[2]

**Incumbent Status Not Decisive**: While Waller's position as an incumbent Fed governor provides some advantage over external candidates, this has not translated into the nomination frontrunner status.[2]

## Timeline and Decision Context

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent indicated on November 25 that President Trump is likely to announce the new Fed Chair before Christmas 2025.[2] Jerome Powell's current term ends in May 2026, giving the administration time to nominate and confirm a successor.[3] The appointment requires presidential nomination followed by Senate confirmation.[2]

## Expert Assessment

Market experts anticipate that Trump will select a candidate who aligns with his stance on lowering interest rates and promoting economic growth.[2] Waller's recent dovish statements and willingness to engage publicly about the position have made him a more visible contender, but Hassett's commanding polling lead and explicit policy alignment with Trump make him the clear favorite at this stage.
