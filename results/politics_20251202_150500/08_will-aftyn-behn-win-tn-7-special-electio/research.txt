# Tennessee's 7th Congressional District Special Election

## Election Overview

Tennessee's 7th congressional district held a special election following the resignation of Republican Mark Green on July 20, 2025, who departed to take a private sector job after voting to help pass the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.

The general election features Republican Matt Van Epps facing Democrat Aftyn Behn, along with four independent candidates: Terri Christie, Bobby Dodge, Robert James Sutherby, and Jon Thorp. Early voting ran from November 12 to November 26, 2025.

## Primary Results

**Republican Primary (October 7)**

Matt Van Epps decisively won the Republican primary with 19,006 votes (51.56%), easily outpacing runner-up Jody Barrett who received 9,337 votes (25.33%). Van Epps demonstrated broad geographic support by winning twelve of the fourteen counties in the district.[1]

**Democratic Primary (October 7)**

The Democratic race was significantly closer. Aftyn Behn, a state representative from the 51st district, narrowly prevailed with 8,653 votes (27.89%), edging out Darden Copeland who received 7,720 votes (24.88%).[1] Behn's victory relied heavily on strong performance in the more populous Montgomery and Davidson counties, while she carried Williamson County by only a slim margin.[1]

## Candidate Profiles

**Aftyn Behn (Democratic Nominee)**

Behn is a sitting state representative with affiliations to progressive organizations including Indivisible Tennessee, Knoxville Democratic Socialists of America, Moms Demand Action, and Progressive Democrats of America.[1] She raised $1,230,629 during the primary campaign.[1]

**Matt Van Epps (Republican Nominee)**

Van Epps received endorsements from Kid Rock and the National Federation of Independent Business FedPAC.[1] His commanding primary victory margin suggests strong support within the Republican base.

## Recent Polling

An Emerson College poll conducted November 22-24, 2025 shows the race extremely competitive:[1]

- Matt Van Epps (R): 48%
- Aftyn Behn (D): 46%
- Other/Undecided: 6%
- Margin of error: ±3.9%

This represents a statistical dead heat, with Van Epps holding only a narrow two-point advantage well within the margin of error.

## District Characteristics

The 7th district leans Republican overall, as evidenced by Van Epps' dominant primary performance across multiple counties. However, the district contains competitive areas, particularly in Montgomery and Davidson counties where Behn performed strongly in the Democratic primary. These more populous urban and suburban areas present Behn's strongest opportunities for victory.

The special election race remains highly competitive as of early December 2025, with polling indicating the outcome could plausibly go either direction.
