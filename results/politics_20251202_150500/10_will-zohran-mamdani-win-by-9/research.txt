**Zohran Mamdani won the 2025 New York City mayoral election with approximately 50.4% of the vote, defeating his closest competitor by roughly an 11% margin, which exceeds the >9% threshold in the prediction market question.** He secured a historic victory powered strongly by youth voters and a record-high total voter turnout for a NYC mayoral race in decades[2][4].

Key data and context include:

- **Vote margins and turnout:** Mamdani won with 50.4% of the overall vote while former governor Andrew Cuomo took about 40%, and Curtis Sliwa trailed. This translates to an estimated margin over 9%, closer to an 11% lead on Cuomo[2][4]. The race attracted over two million voters total—the highest since 1969[2][4]. Mamdani also became the first NYC mayoral candidate since 1969 to surpass one million votes[2].

- **Youth voter influence:** Young voters (18-29 years old) played a decisive role, providing overwhelming support. They made up 11% of all ballots, with a turnout of 28%, significantly higher than past municipal elections[1]. Youth voted 75% in favor of Mamdani versus only 19% for Cuomo and 5% for Sliwa[1]. Within this group, youth of color and young women were particularly strong supporters (up to 85% Latino youth and 83% Black youth backing Mamdani)[1].

- **Platform and political context:** Mamdani ran on a democratic socialist platform emphasizing affordability, contrasting Cuomo’s centrist, law-and-order-focused campaign and Sliwa’s right-leaning tough-on-crime stance[2]. Mamdani's campaign successfully energized left and youth coalitions.

- **Primary election results:** In the Democratic primary, Mamdani led convincingly with 56.4% of votes in the final round against Cuomo's 36.2%, demonstrating strong intra-party support prior to the general election[3].

- **Expert observations:** Analysts and exit polls noted Mamdani’s ability to mobilize unusual youth turnout and diverse coalitions, countering traditional assumptions that Cuomo would dominate younger voter segments. His victory is considered an outlier that revitalized civic engagement after decades of declining local election turnout[1][4].

Factors influencing the outcome included:
- The surge in **young voter registration and turnout**.
- The appeal of Mamdani’s **affordability and progressive platform** to urban youth and minority communities.
- A split in the electorate among three major candidates, weakening Cuomo’s advantage.
- Historical context of declining local election engagement reversed this cycle in 2025.

Overall, the data strongly support that Zohran Mamdani won by more than 9%, with youth turnout and coalition-building being critical to his success[1][2][4].
